Who can see recordings?
Only authorized moderators on the server, according to the role configuration and channel access settings.
Can you prevent a member from using /record?
Yes. Moderators can apply targeted restrictions to block the recording request for specific users.
Are temporary voice channels cleaned up automatically?
Yes. As soon as the last member leaves, the temporary channel is removed to keep the server clean and readable.
